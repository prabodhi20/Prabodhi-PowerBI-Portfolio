## Business Issue

TechHome's sales data was spread across multiple dimensions, making it difficult to evaluate revenue, profitability, discount impact, and customer purchasing behavior. Business stakeholders needed a centralized reporting solution to monitor performance across products, categories, regions, customer segments, and shipping methods.

---

## Resolution

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard using Power Query, DAX, and data modeling to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

The solution includes:

- Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query
- Interactive KPI reporting
- Profitability and sales trend analysis
- Discount impact analysis
- Customer segment and shipping analysis
- Dynamic conditional formatting
- Interactive filtering

---

## Business Impact

The dashboard enables management to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer segments, shipping trends, and discount effectiveness from a centralized view, supporting better pricing, sales, and business decisions.


## Business Questions Answered

- Which product categories generate the highest profit?
- Which sub-categories are loss-making?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which customer segment contributes the highest sales?
- Which shipping mode is most frequently used?
- How do sales and profit change over time?

---

## Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides a centralized view of sales performance and profitability using interactive KPIs and business visualizations.

### Key Performance Indicators

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Orders
- Profit Status (Dynamic KPI with conditional formatting)

### Key Visualizations

- Total Profit and Total Orders by Year
- Most Used Shipping Mode
- Profit by Category
- Profit by Product Sub-Category
- Discount Impact on Profit Margin % vs Total Profit
- Sales by Customer Segment

## Data Preparation (Power Query)

Power Query was used to prepare the dataset for analysis.

Key transformations included:

- Standardized data types
- Cleaned null and blank values
- Removed unnecessary columns
- Renamed columns for consistency
- Created calculated and conditional columns
- Created a dedicated Date table
- Optimized the data model for reporting

### KPI Measures

- Profit Status

### Analytical Measures

- Dynamic Titles
- Conditional Formatting
- Sales Trend Analysis
- Profitability Analysis

---

## Key Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest overall profit.
- Higher discount levels were associated with lower profit margins.
- Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping mode.
- Consumer segment contributed the highest share of total sales.
- Furniture, particularly Bookcases, recorded lower profitability and recurring losses.
